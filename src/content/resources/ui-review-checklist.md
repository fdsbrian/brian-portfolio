## Visual hierarchy

- Is the primary action immediately identifiable?
- Are headings clearly differentiated from body content?
- Is important information placed before supporting detail?
- Does the page remain understandable when scanned quickly?

## Spacing and layout

- Is spacing based on a consistent system?
- Are related elements visually grouped?
- Is there sufficient whitespace around key content?
- Does the layout remain balanced across screen sizes?

## Interaction states

- Do interactive elements include hover, focus, active and disabled states?
- Are loading and empty states accounted for?
- Is user feedback provided after important actions?
- Are destructive actions clearly distinguished?

## Accessibility

- Can all interactive elements be reached using a keyboard?
- Are focus indicators clearly visible?
- Do text and interface elements meet appropriate contrast requirements?
- Do meaningful images include descriptive alternative text?

## Responsive behavior

- Does the interface work at mobile, tablet and desktop widths?
- Does content remain readable without horizontal scrolling?
- Do touch targets remain large enough on smaller devices?
- Are complex layouts simplified appropriately on mobile?
